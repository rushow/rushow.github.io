Research

Keeping machine learning models accurate when data changes

My MSc thesis at the University of Regina, Learner-Based Concept Drift Detection Algorithms: A Comprehensive Analysis and Evaluation, focused on concept drift and fraud detection. Fraud patterns keep changing, so a model trained on yesterday’s data can miss today’s fraud. I studied how drift detectors catch these changes, building Python experiments that compare statistical (SPC-based), window-based and ensemble-based detectors on synthetic and real-world data streams with abrupt and gradual drift.

In Italy, at the ELEDIA Research Center, I worked on deep learning for cultural heritage image classification using ensembles of ResNet models. My MSc thesis at the University of Trento studied learning theories and gamification approaches for online education, assessment and training of elderly people.
